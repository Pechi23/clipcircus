How we make our shorts

We watch the streamWe follow big streamers' live streams and pick the moments that make people laugh, gasp or cheer.
We cut and editEach short is our own edit: a vertical frame on the streamer, captions you can read with the sound off, a title that says what happens.
Credit, alwaysEvery post names the streamer and links the original stream, so viewers can watch the whole thing and support the creator.
Clipping campaignsMany streamers and brands invite clippers through clipping campaigns (for example on Content Rewards by Whop). We clip for those campaigns and follow each one's rules.

Our app: autoclip

autoclip is the desktop tool we built to make and publish Clip Circus shorts. It runs on our own computer and is used only by the Clip Circus team for the Clip Circus accounts.

What it does

  • Finds the best moments of a stream we choose, cuts them into vertical shorts and adds captions.
  • Shows every short to us for review. Nothing is posted until we approve it.
  • Posts the shorts we approve to our own YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X and TikTok accounts through each platform's official API, with the title, caption, credit and link to the original stream.
  • Shows us how our posts are doing (views, likes) on a private dashboard.

TikTok

autoclip connects to our TikTok account with TikTok's Login Kit and posts with TikTok's Content Posting API. For every video we see which TikTok account it will be posted to, and we choose who can see it, whether people can comment, duet or stitch, and whether the video promotes a brand, before we approve it. Videos are posted as we edited them, without added watermarks or promotional text.
