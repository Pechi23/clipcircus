Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

Who we are

Clip Circus makes short clips of live streams and posts them on its own social media accounts. "autoclip" is the desktop app Clip Circus uses to make and publish those clips. This policy explains what information autoclip and this website handle and how.

Who uses autoclip

autoclip is used only by the Clip Circus team, on our own computer, to manage the Clip Circus accounts. It is not offered to the public and does not create accounts for other people.

What we collect, and why

From the platforms we connect (TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, X)

  • Account basics of our own connected accounts: name, username, profile picture and account ID, to show which account a video will be posted to.
  • Access tokens the platform gives when we log in, so autoclip can post the videos we approve. They are kept only on our computer.
  • Posting options the platform offers for our account (for TikTok: who can see a video, whether comments, duets and stitches are allowed, the longest video allowed), to show them on the posting screen.
  • Numbers about our own posts (views, likes, comments, shares), to see how our clips do.

From this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or trackers and does not collect any personal information. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in its own privacy statement.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell, rent or share any of this information with anyone.
  • We do not collect information about viewers of our videos or about other users of these platforms.
  • We do not use this information for advertising or to train AI models.

Where it is kept, and for how long

Everything autoclip handles stays in files on our own computer. Access tokens are kept until we disconnect an account or the platform ends them. Post numbers are kept while we keep the posts. Disconnecting an account in autoclip deletes its tokens.

Your choices

The only accounts autoclip connects to are Clip Circus's own. Access can be removed at any time by disconnecting the account in autoclip or by removing the app's access in the platform's own settings (for TikTok: Settings and privacy ▸ Security ▸ Manage app permissions).

Children

autoclip and this website are not meant for children under 13 and do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version is posted on this page with a new date. Questions or requests: message @clipcircushq on Instagram or X.
